Finding a Psychologist in Newcastle

Newcastle and the broader Hunter Region has a growing network of registered psychologists offering a wide range of services — from anxiety and depression through to specialist eating disorder treatment, ADHD assessment, and trauma therapy.

Most psychologists in Newcastle accept referrals under a Mental Health Care Plan (MHCP) from your GP, which entitles you to Medicare rebates on up to 10 individual sessions per calendar year.

Telehealth options have expanded significantly, meaning you can access Newcastle-based psychologists from anywhere across NSW — useful for clients in the Hunter Valley, Maitland, and Lake Macquarie areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a GP referral to see a psychologist?
No — you can self-refer as a private client. However, a GP referral with a Mental Health Care Plan unlocks Medicare rebates, reducing your out-of-pocket costs significantly.
What's the difference between a psychologist and a psychiatrist?
Psychologists are trained in assessment and therapy. Psychiatrists are medical doctors who can prescribe medication. Both treat mental health conditions but through different means.
How much does a session cost in Newcastle?
Private sessions typically range from $180–$280 per hour. With a Medicare rebate under an MHCP, you receive back approximately $141.85 (clinical) or $96.65 (registered) per session.
Can I access telehealth psychology sessions from Newcastle?
Yes. Most Newcastle psychologists now offer telehealth (video) sessions, attracting the same Medicare rebates as in-person sessions when conducted under an MHCP.
How do I verify a psychologist is AHPRA registered?
You can search any practitioner's registration at ahpra.gov.au using their name. Registration must be current and active for them to legally practice in Australia.
