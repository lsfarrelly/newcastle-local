About Broadmeadow Medical Centre

One of Newcastle's largest non-corporate medical centres. Broadmeadow Medical Centre has on-site pharmacy, physiotherapy, pathology, and a dedicated skin cancer clinic. Mixed billing with bulk billing available at the doctor's discretion for concession card holders and children.
