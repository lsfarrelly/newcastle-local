About Grant Lyall Psychology

Grant Lyall Psychology Services is an East Maitland practice with over 10 years of experience supporting children, adolescents and adults through complex psychological, cognitive and developmental challenges. Located near Green Hills Shopping Centre, the practice provides individual therapy, psychological assessments and support for anxiety, depression, autism, learning disorders and ADHD.
