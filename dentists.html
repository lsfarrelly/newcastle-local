Finding a Dentist in Newcastle

Newcastle has a strong network of dental practices ranging from family-friendly clinics to specialist cosmetic dentists. Whether you need a routine check-up, teeth whitening, Invisalign, or urgent emergency dental care, there's a local dentist ready to help across Newcastle CBD, Merewether, Hamilton, Kotara, and surrounding suburbs.

Many Newcastle dentists are preferred providers for major health funds including HCF, CBHS, Medibank, and NIB — always confirm your fund status before booking to maximise your rebate. Extended hours and Saturday appointments are increasingly common, particularly in shopping centres and medical precincts.

Emergency dental care is available from several Newcastle practices, with same-day appointments often offered for dental pain, broken teeth, and other urgent needs. Cosmetic dentistry options including Invisalign, veneers, and implants are widely available across the region.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a dentist accepting new patients in Newcastle?
Call ahead or check the practice website — many Newcastle dental practices note their availability online. Our directory shows which practices are currently accepting new patients.
Are Newcastle dentists covered by health funds?
Most Newcastle dental practices accept major health funds including HCF, Medibank, CBHS, and NIB. Some are preferred providers which can increase your rebate. Always confirm with the practice before booking.
What should I do for a dental emergency in Newcastle?
Call a dental practice that offers emergency appointments — several Newcastle dentists offer same-day emergency slots for pain, broken teeth, or lost fillings. If after hours, some practices have emergency phone lines.
How much does a dental check-up cost in Newcastle?
A standard check-up and clean typically costs $180-$280 in Newcastle without health insurance. With a major health fund, your out-of-pocket cost is often reduced significantly. Some practices also offer no-gap check-ups for eligible fund members.
