Finding an Electrician in Newcastle

Newcastle and the Hunter region has a large pool of licensed electricians covering everything from basic home repairs and switchboard upgrades to solar installation, data cabling, and large commercial projects. Always verify a licence number before booking any electrical work.

In NSW, all electrical work must be performed by a licensed electrician. You can verify any electrician's licence at the NSW Fair Trading licence check tool. Level 2 ASP (Accredited Service Provider) electricians are additionally authorised to work on the electrical network.

For electrical emergencies including power outages, tripped circuits, or electrical hazards, several Newcastle electricians offer 24/7 emergency callout services. Always use a licensed professional — DIY electrical work is illegal in NSW and carries serious safety risks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check an electrician's licence in NSW?
Visit the NSW Fair Trading licence check at fairtrading.nsw.gov.au and search by name or licence number. All licensed electricians must provide their licence number on quotes and invoices.
What is a Level 2 ASP electrician?
A Level 2 Accredited Service Provider is authorised to work on the electrical network — the wires between the street and your meter. You need one for new power connections, meter upgrades, and some solar installations.
Do Newcastle electricians charge a call-out fee?
Most do charge a call-out or service fee, especially for after-hours emergency work. The fee typically ranges from $80-$150 for standard hours and more for evenings and weekends. Always confirm before booking.
Can an electrician install solar panels in Newcastle?
A licensed electrician can connect and commission solar systems, but the panel installation itself requires a Clean Energy Council accredited installer. Many Newcastle electricians hold both qualifications.
