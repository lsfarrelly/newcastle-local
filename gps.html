Finding a GP in Newcastle

Newcastle and the Hunter Region has a well-established network of GP practices covering everything from routine check-ups to complex chronic disease management. Whether you need a bulk billing practice or a mixed billing clinic with extended hours, there are good options across the suburbs.

Most Newcastle practices operate Monday to Friday, with several offering Saturday morning appointments. Newcastle After Hours Medical Service (NAHMS) provides after-hours consultations and a home doctor service in the evenings and on weekends.

When choosing a GP, consider whether the practice is accepting new patients, their billing policy, and proximity to home or work. Continuity of care — seeing the same doctor regularly — is particularly important for managing chronic conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is bulk billing?
Bulk billing means the doctor charges Medicare directly and you pay nothing out of pocket. Mixed billing practices charge a gap fee above the Medicare rebate. Always confirm billing policy when booking.
How do I find a GP accepting new patients in Newcastle?
Use the "Accepting New Patients" filter above to see current availability. You can also call ahead as availability changes frequently — practices that were full may open up without updating directories.
What is the after-hours GP service in Newcastle?
Newcastle After Hours Medical Service (NAHMS) operates evenings and weekends from Broadmeadow, and also offers a mobile home doctor service. It is fully bulk billed for Medicare card holders.
Can I get a telehealth appointment with a Newcastle GP?
Most practices now offer telehealth (phone or video) consultations, particularly for follow-ups and repeat prescriptions. You generally need to have had an in-person appointment at the practice first to be eligible.
