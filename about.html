About Newcastle Local

The Hunter Region's most detailed local business directory — built to help you find the right service, first time.

What is Newcastle Local?

Newcastle Local is an independent online directory dedicated to local businesses and service providers across the Newcastle, Hunter Valley, Lake Macquarie, Maitland, and Port Stephens regions of New South Wales.

Unlike large national directories that aggregate thousands of listings across Australia with little local focus, Newcastle Local is built specifically for the Hunter Region. Every listing is verified to be genuinely local, and every profile is designed to give you the detailed information you need — hours, services, contact details, billing information, and more — so you can make a confident decision without endless clicking.

Whether you need a bulk-billing GP, a licensed electrician, a family lawyer, or an NDIS support coordinator, Newcastle Local is your starting point.

Why we built it

Finding a reliable local service provider in Newcastle shouldn't be hard. But searching Google or large national directories often returns a mix of results from Sydney, franchises with no local presence, or outdated contact information. Finding a psychologist who is actually accepting new clients, a GP who bulk bills, or an electrician who services your suburb — that often requires multiple calls and wasted time.

Newcastle Local was created to solve this. Our directory focuses on depth over breadth: each listing includes detailed service information, availability status, contact details, and direct links so you can act immediately. Business owners can update their own listings at any time, so information stays current.

The region we serve

All listings on Newcastle Local are sourced from businesses operating within approximately 60 kilometres of Newcastle CBD (postcode 2300). This includes:

Newcastle City Newcastle CBD, Hamilton, Merewether, Charlestown, Adamstown, Mayfield, Wallsend, Jesmond
Lake Macquarie Cardiff, Glendale, Warners Bay, Belmont, Redhead, Toronto, Swansea, Morisset

Service categories

Newcastle Local currently covers the following categories, with more being added regularly:

  • Health — GPs (including bulk billing), Psychologists, Physiotherapists, Dentists
  • Trades — Plumbers, Electricians, Builders
  • Legal — Family lawyers, criminal lawyers, conveyancers, and general solicitors
  • Finance — Accountants, tax agents, business advisors
  • NDIS — Registered NDIS providers, support coordinators, allied health
  • Automotive — Mechanics, mobile mechanics, car servicing
  • Removalists — Local and interstate moving services

Each category includes detailed filter options relevant to that service type — for example, bulk billing status for GPs, emergency availability for electricians, or mobile service for mechanics.

How listings work

Listings on Newcastle Local are created from publicly available information and direct research. Every business listed is a real, operating local business. Information is verified at the time of listing and updated regularly.

Business owners can claim and manage their listing for free. After verifying ownership with a unique secret key, business owners can update their contact details, availability status, services, and other information at any time. Updates go live automatically within approximately 60 seconds.

If you notice incorrect information about a listing, or if you are a business owner who would like to update or remove your listing, please contact us and we will respond promptly.

Our commitment to the Hunter Region

Newcastle Local is not a national platform trying to cover every city. We are focused entirely on the Hunter Region — the area we know, the community we're part of, and the local economy we want to support.

Local businesses are the backbone of the Newcastle and Hunter economy. They employ local people, sponsor local events, and contribute to the character of the region's suburbs and neighbourhoods. Newcastle Local exists to make those businesses easier to find, so more of the region's spending stays local.

We are committed to keeping Newcastle Local free for consumers and free for basic business listings.
