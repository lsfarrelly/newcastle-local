Finding a Plumber in Newcastle

Newcastle and the Hunter Region has a strong network of licensed plumbers covering everything from emergency callouts to major renovations. Whether you need a blocked drain cleared at 2am or a new hot water system installed, there's a local plumber ready to help.

All plumbers listed here hold a current NSW plumbing licence. When hiring, always ask to see their licence number and check it at the NSW Fair Trading register.

For emergency plumbing, most Newcastle plumbers offer 24/7 callout service with no extra call-out fee. Pipe relining specialists can fix damaged drains without excavation — worth asking about if you have a recurring blockage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check a plumber's licence in NSW?
Search any plumber by name or licence number at the NSW Fair Trading licence check tool at fairtrading.nsw.gov.au. All licensed plumbers must display their licence number on invoices.
What's the difference between pipe relining and pipe replacement?
Pipe relining inserts a resin-coated liner into your existing pipe, curing it in place — no digging required. It's typically faster and cheaper than full pipe replacement for most residential drain problems.
Do Newcastle plumbers charge a call-out fee?
It varies — some plumbers advertise no call-out fees, while others charge a fixed fee for the first visit. Always confirm before booking, especially for after-hours emergency work.
Can a plumber do gas fitting work in NSW?
Yes, but only if they hold a gas fitting endorsement on their plumbing licence. Not all licensed plumbers are also licensed for gas — check before booking gas work.
