1. Privacy Policy

Newcastle Local (we, us, our) is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) (Privacy Act) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) contained in Schedule 1 of that Act. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, hold, use, and disclose personal information.

1.1 What information we collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Business listing information submitted by business owners, including business name, address, phone number, email address, and service descriptions.
  • Contact information provided when you contact us by email, including your name and email address.
  • Technical data automatically collected when you visit the Website, including IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited, and session duration. This information is collected via server logs and, where applicable, third-party analytics tools (see section 1.6).
  • Secret keys issued to business owners for listing management. These are alphanumeric tokens associated with a business listing and do not, by themselves, constitute personal information.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information as defined under APP 3.3 (including health information, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, or criminal record) unless it is voluntarily provided by business owners in the context of describing their services (for example, a health service provider describing the health conditions they treat).

1.2 How we collect information

We collect personal information:

  • Directly from you when you submit a business listing, contact us by email, or use listing management tools on the Website;
  • From publicly available sources including business websites, Google Business Profile, and industry directories, for the purpose of creating or verifying directory listings; and
  • Automatically through your use of the Website (technical data).

Where we have collected your information from a publicly available source, we will, upon request, inform you of the source from which your information was obtained.

1.3 Why we collect and use information

We collect and use personal information for the following primary purposes:

  • To publish and maintain business listings on the Website;
  • To allow business owners to update or remove their listings;
  • To respond to enquiries and feedback;
  • To improve the Website's content, features, and user experience; and
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

We will not use your personal information for a secondary purpose unless you have consented, or we are permitted or required to do so by law.

1.4 Disclosure of personal information

Business listing information (including business name, address, phone number, and email addresses voluntarily provided for directory display) is published publicly on the Website. This is the core purpose for which the information is collected from business owners, and by submitting a listing, business owners consent to this publication.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

We may disclose personal information to:

  • Third-party service providers who assist us in operating the Website (including hosting providers and analytics providers), where those providers are contractually required to handle information in accordance with the APPs; and
  • Law enforcement or government agencies where we are required by law to do so.

We are not likely to disclose personal information to overseas recipients. If we are required to do so, we will take reasonable steps to ensure the overseas recipient complies with the APPs, or we will seek your consent.

1.5 Storage and security

Personal information collected through the Website is stored in our hosting infrastructure operated by Netlify Inc. (United States) and data storage services operated by GitHub Inc. (United States). By using the Website, you acknowledge that your information may be processed and stored outside Australia.

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure, including by using access controls and limiting the information we collect to what is necessary.

Despite our efforts, no data transmission over the internet or data storage system is fully secure. We cannot guarantee the security of information transmitted to or from the Website.

1.6 Cookies and analytics

The Website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies. Cookies are small text files placed on your device to assist with Website functionality and to gather usage statistics. We do not use cookies to track individuals across unaffiliated websites.

If we use a third-party analytics tool (such as Google Analytics), that provider's privacy policy will govern the collection and use of data by that tool. You may opt out of analytics tracking by using browser extensions or settings provided by the analytics provider.

1.7 Links to third-party websites

The Website may contain links to external websites, including business websites listed in our directory. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any external site you visit.

1.8 Accessing and correcting your information

Under the Privacy Act, you have the right to access personal information we hold about you and to request correction of that information if it is inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date. To make a request, please contact us using the details in section 5. We will respond to access and correction requests within a reasonable time (ordinarily within 30 days).

In limited circumstances, we may refuse access to personal information. If we refuse, we will provide written reasons unless it is unreasonable to do so.

1.9 Retention and deletion

We retain personal information for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Business listing information is retained while the listing is active. When a listing is removed, associated personal information is deleted from our systems within a reasonable time. You may request deletion of your information by contacting us.

1.10 Children's privacy

The Website is not directed at children under the age of 15 years and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information.
